When pop icons Miley Cyrus and Dua Lipa collaborated to release “Prisoner” in 2020, the hit song immediately topped the charts in their respective homelands of the United States and the United Kingdom. But when the song is played on AM/FM radio in the U.K., Dua Lipa receives royalties for her performance and Miley Cyrus does not. The U.K.
